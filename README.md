# Streamside Fly Picker

Picks trout flies from what you see on the water and keeps track of your fly box. Lost flies stay out of rotation until you replace them.

Open it once with signal and add it to your home screen. After that it works with no signal at all. Your fly box is saved on your phone only; use "Back up or move your fly box" to keep a copy.

`app.html` is the source. `index.html` is the same page wrapped for hosting.
